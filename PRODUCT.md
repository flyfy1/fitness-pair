# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Kids and adults who want a fun way to be more active at home, entering an arcade from a landing page or a shared clip.

## Product Purpose
Make moving fun through small camera-controlled games. The primary landing-page action is “Take me to the arcade”.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Motion Quest maps calibrated squats to five attacks. Dino Run has an experimental calibrated jump-height input. Recognition stays on device. Keyboard demonstrations and future-game simulations must be clearly labeled.
Recording is opt-in for one round at a time. Exported clips include the Hopmodo logo, name, game score, and canonical website. Native file sharing is available where supported, with download and copy-game-link fallbacks. Finished clips remain device-local until a separate explicit sharing action. The requested destination is a GCP bucket; its project, bucket and credentials are pending the owner. Public gallery and individual share pages bring visitors back to the arcade. Do not promise that playable videos cannot be copied.

## Brand Commitments
Public-facing name: Hopmodo, with a cobalt jumping-player mark. Repository, package names, Sites app name, and public URL remain unchanged.
Plain, friendly English. Explain what the game does and how to start. Avoid vague slogans, fitness guarantees, and jokey error messages. Bold, highly creative kinetic playground direction, selected by the user: cobalt lettering, acid-yellow playfield, responsive game scenes. Include a factual section explaining how the project was built with Astra.

## Evidence on Hand
Repository games, contracts, synthetic and browser checks. Human recognition accuracy, exercise quality, health outcomes and enjoyment remain unverified. No invented players, testimonials or live gallery entries.

## Product Principles
- Show playable mechanisms and make the arcade easy to enter.
- Preserve independent game ownership and the named-joint contracts.
- Obtain recording and sharing consent separately.
- Distinguish working games, experiments and simulated concepts.

## Open Decisions
GCP project and bucket; eventual public audience configuration; additional verified examples of Astra's contribution beyond this build.
